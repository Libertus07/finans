## 2025-02-15 - Intl API Performance Bottleneck
**Learning:** Repeatedly instantiating `Intl.NumberFormat` and calling `toLocaleDateString()` inside frequently called formatting helpers (`formatCurrency`, `formatDate`) causes significant overhead (~56x and ~35x slower respectively). The `toLocaleDateString` method under the hood does something similar to `Intl.DateTimeFormat`.
**Action:** Always instantiate `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` once at the module level and reuse them for formatting to achieve ~30x-60x performance gains. When optimizing `toLocaleDateString` to `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, remember to add a valid date check (`isNaN(date)`) since `Intl.DateTimeFormat().format(new Date('invalid'))` throws a RangeError whereas `toLocaleDateString` handles it gracefully.
