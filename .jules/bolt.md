## 2024-05-18 - Cached Intl Formatters and Invalid Dates
**Learning:** Instantiating `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside utility functions like `formatCurrency` and `formatDate` causes significant overhead (up to ~50x slower) because they are re-created on every call (e.g. during large list renders). Caching them at the module level provides a massive performance boost. However, when switching `toLocaleDateString()` to a cached `Intl.DateTimeFormat().format()`, note that the latter throws a `RangeError: Invalid time value` for invalid dates, whereas `toLocaleDateString` just returns "Invalid Date".
**Action:** Always extract `Intl` formatters to module-level constants. When refactoring date formatters, explicitly handle invalid dates (e.g., `isNaN(date)`) to avoid runtime crashes.
