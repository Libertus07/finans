## 2025-02-12 - Date and Number Formatting Instantiation Bottleneck
**Learning:** In heavily re-rendered UI components (like tables, dashboards, and POS systems) that format currency and dates frequently, repeatedly instantiating `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside utility functions creates a measurable performance bottleneck. Benchmarks showed caching these instances speeds up `formatCurrency` by ~68x (from ~6966ms to ~101ms for 100k calls) and `formatDate` by ~56x (from ~20467ms to ~363ms for 100k calls).
**Action:** Extract `Intl` object instantiations outside of the function scope into module-level constants in `src/utils/helpers.js` to reuse them across calls. Also remember to handle invalid dates properly when refactoring `new Date(...).toLocaleDateString(...)` to `Intl.DateTimeFormat().format(...)`, as the latter throws an error on invalid dates while the former gracefully returns "Invalid Date".
